ABOUT SHRI RAM PUBLIC SCHOOL

  • The school was established in 2004.
  • Shri Ram Public School is a Co-ed school affiliated to Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).
  • It is managed by Bal Sewa Sadan.
    • SHRI RAM PUBLIC SCHOOL has been established in 2004 to immortalize the memory and contribution of late Shri Ram Rastogi, the founder of the prestigious Swadeshi group of industries whose benevolence and philanthropy always found expression in serving humanity across various sections of society, with the vision, mission and dedication to provide the children a total quality education with modern tools and strategy in the mould of Indian values and ethics. The school is intended to meet the twin objectives of corporate social responsibilities and facilitate quality schooling. The school is committed not only to impart quality education but also the all-round development of the children to make them ideal citizen of the new millennium.
